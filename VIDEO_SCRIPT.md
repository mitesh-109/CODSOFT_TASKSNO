# 60–90 Second CodSoft Demo Video Script

Hello, I am Mitesh. This is my CodSoft Data Science internship project repository.

I completed three tasks: Titanic Survival Prediction, Iris Flower Classification, and Sales Prediction using Python.

For the Titanic task, I performed preprocessing and trained a Logistic Regression classification model to predict survival.

For the Iris task, I used sepal and petal measurements to classify the three Iris species and evaluated the model with standard classification metrics.

For the Sales Prediction task, I used advertising expenditure data and Linear Regression to estimate sales, then evaluated the predictions using MAE, RMSE and R-squared.

The projects use Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Matplotlib and Seaborn. Thank you for watching.
